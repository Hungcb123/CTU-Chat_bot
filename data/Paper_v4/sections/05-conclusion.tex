\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper addressed knowledge heterogeneity in university counseling by introducing \system{}, a centralized supervisor-routed multi-agent architecture coordinating four domain specialists with isolated evidence spaces and asymmetric tool privileges. By allocating relational curricula to Neo4j, statutory tuition to structured catalogs with deterministic arithmetic tools, and narrative decrees to hybrid RAG (BM25, dense embeddings, RRF, and neural reranking), \system{} eliminates the uniform representation bottleneck and decision-space pollution. Empirical evaluations on an official Can Tho University benchmark substantiate this design: attaining 97.0\% supervisor routing accuracy with 100.0\% bounded safety, superior retrieval performance (0.9600 Hit@1, 0.9700 MRR@10), and top factual exact match (62.03\% Fact EM) in end-to-end generation. Future work will investigate cross-institutional adaptation, controlled computational-efficiency evaluation and model compression, and live longitudinal student trials.
