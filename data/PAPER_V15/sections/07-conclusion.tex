\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluated \system{}, a supervisor-routed multi-agent RAG system for university counseling, through architecture comparison, component ablations, and tool ambiguity tests. Its Automated Fact Pass Rate point estimate exceeds the matched Single Agent by 10.0\,pp (paired 95\% CI $[+2.0, +19.0]$\,pp; exact McNemar $p{=}0.0414$), with fewer recorded specialist input tokens. Removing specialist policies or graph evidence lowers pass rate or Source Recall, respectively. At 51 tools, Routed Specialist records 88.0\% tool E2E Success. The deterministic calculator yields more tuition exact matches (8/9 vs.\ 3/9 without), while scoped tool access bounds the specialist's visible registry in the 51-tool test. These findings concern automated fact matching and controlled tool cases; live multi-turn benefit and comparative end-to-end speed remain untested. Future work will reduce the measured $10.65$\,s pipeline latency and evaluate transfer to other institutions.
