\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluated \system{}, a supervisor-routed multi-agent RAG system for university counseling, through architecture comparison, component ablations, and tool ambiguity tests. Its Automated Fact Pass Rate point estimate exceeds the matched Single Agent by 10.0\,pp (paired 95\% CI $[+2.0, +19.0]$\,pp; exact McNemar $p{=}0.0414$), with fewer recorded specialist input tokens. Removing specialist policies or graph evidence lowers pass rate or Source Recall, respectively. At 51 tools, Routed Specialist records 88.0\% tool E2E Success. Deterministic calculation ablation yields an estimated $-4.0$\,pp difference in overall fact pass (95\% CI $[-9.0, 0.0]$\,pp), while scoped tool access bounds the specialist's visible registry in the 51-tool test. These findings reflect automated fact matching and controlled tool cases; live multi-turn benefits remain untested. Future work will address the $10.65$\,s pipeline latency and evaluate transfer to other institutions.
