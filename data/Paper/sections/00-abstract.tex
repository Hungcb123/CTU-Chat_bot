\begin{abstract}
University administrative question answering demands more than
semantic similarity matching: answers must respect document validity,
organizational authority, temporal scope, and exact procedural or
numerical rules.
This paper presents {\system{}}, a bilingual Vietnamese--English
framework that integrates metadata-filtered \mbox{BM25} and dense retrieval,
a provenance-preserving knowledge graph, cross-encoder evidence
reranking, and a specialist-agent workflow.
PostgreSQL serves as the authoritative data store, while Qdrant and
Neo4j function as rebuildable retrieval projections; every candidate
is revalidated against publication, approval, audience, category, and
effective-date constraints prior to generation.
The framework is evaluated through retrieval and end-to-end QA studies
using 150 human-reviewed questions spanning nine administrative categories.
The full retrieval configuration achieves H@1 of 0.8667, perfect H@3 and
Recall@5, and MRR@10 of 0.9222. In the end-to-end evaluation, the proposed
system attains Context Recall of 0.745 and Context Precision of 0.749, while
removing governance produces the lowest Answer Correctness score.
This work provides a reproducible design for evidence-grounded
administrative assistants in low-resource bilingual settings.

\end{abstract}
