\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluated \system{}, a supervisor-routed multi-agent RAG system for university counseling, across architecture comparisons, ablations, and registry scaling tests. Under lexical and LLM judging, \system{} surpasses the matched Single Agent in fact pass rate (+10.0\,pp lexical, +4.0\,pp strict semantic, +2.5\,pp numeric match) with 41.4\% fewer specialist input tokens. Ablations demonstrate that deterministic calculators and curriculum graphs are vital for mitigating arithmetic and prerequisite hallucinations. At 51 tools, scoped specialist isolation preserves 88.0\% tool E2E Success. Future work will optimize the $10.65$\,s latency and assess institutional transfer.
