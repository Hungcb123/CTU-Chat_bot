\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluated \system{}, a supervisor-routed multi-agent RAG system for university counseling, across architecture comparisons, ablations, and tool scaling. In architecture comparisons, \system{} surpasses the Single Agent in lexical fact pass (+10.0\,pp, $p{=}0.0414$) with 41.4\% fewer specialist input tokens, while exhibiting comparable semantic accuracy (57.7\% vs.\ 58.6\% Mean AC; 37.0\% vs.\ 33.0\% strict pass, $p{=}0.4807$) and higher financial numeric exact match (62.5\% vs.\ 60.0\%). Ablations confirm that deterministic calculators and curriculum graphs mitigate arithmetic and prerequisite errors. At 51 tools, specialist isolation maintains 88.0\% tool E2E Success. Future work will address dispatch latency ($10.65$\,s) and institutional transfer.
