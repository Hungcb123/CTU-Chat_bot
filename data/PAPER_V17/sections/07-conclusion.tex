\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We evaluated \system{} for university counseling through architecture comparisons, ablations, and tool ambiguity tests. Automated Fact Pass Rate exceeds the matched Single Agent by 10.0\,pp (paired 95\% CI $[+2.0, +19.0]$\,pp; exact McNemar $p{=}0.0414$), with fewer specialist input tokens. Removing specialist policies or graph evidence lowers pass rate or Source Recall, respectively. At 51 tools, Routed Specialist records 88.0\% tool E2E Success. Deterministic calculation ablation yields an estimated $-4.0$\,pp difference in overall fact pass (95\% CI $[-9.0, 0.0]$\,pp); scoped access also bounds visible tools at 51-tool scale. To address offline fact-matching limitations, future work aims to combine advisor reviews, LLM-as-a-judge frameworks, and deterministic calculations. We also plan token streaming, caching, and prompt refinement to mitigate pipeline latency. Finally, student pilot trials will collect live colloquial queries for advisor annotation to expand the benchmark, while evaluating institutional portability.
